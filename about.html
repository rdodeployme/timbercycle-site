About

Specialists, not
generalists.

Timbercycle is a Recycle Group company that does one thing: recover timber, starting with pallets, and keep it out of landfill by finding it somewhere else to go.

Why timber only

Narrow beats broad.

Plenty of operators will take a bit of everything and do none of it particularly well. We've gone the other way. Timber is the whole business — which means the plant, the sorting, the grading and the end markets are all built around it.

That's also why we can say yes to volumes that general waste contractors turn down, and why we can take the difficult material as well as the easy material.

Where we operate

Victoria first.
The rest to follow.

Our site is at 116 Furner Avenue, North Geelong. Victoria is our flagship market and it's where our plant, our people and our processing capacity are.

Other states are on the plan, and Recycle Group already operates beyond Victoria. We'll list a market here when we're genuinely servicing it — not before. If you're outside Victoria and sitting on serious pallet volume, get in touch anyway and we'll tell you where we're up to.

Research, development & innovation

Working on the timbers nobody has solved.

Some timber has an obvious recycling pathway. Some doesn't, and it goes to landfill by default. We put work into the second category — improving processing techniques, trialling treatment methods, and looking for end markets that make recovery viable at scale.

Difficult streams

Laminated timbers, particle board and MDF, painted and treated timbers, composite and engineered wood products.

What we bring

Real operating sites, consistent material volumes, and honest data from trials that either worked or didn't.

Who we work with

Manufacturers, technology providers, universities and research bodies, councils, and construction and demolition operators.

The test

Whether a pathway can be measured, repeated and scaled. If it can't, it isn't a solution yet.

Part of Recycle Group

Infrastructure behind the promise.

Timbercycle operates within Recycle Group, alongside the group's other recovery businesses. That gives us established sites, transport capability and processing capacity — the things that decide whether a recycling commitment survives contact with a real volume of material.

Each brand in the group stays in its own lane. Ours is timber.
