Two streams

One collection. Two outcomes.

01

Reuse

A pallet is an asset until someone decides it isn't. We collect, sort and grade, repair what's structurally sound, and put it back into the supply chain.

Pallet recovery & reuse 02

Recycle

Anything past repair gets shredded. It comes back as mulch, oversize mulch, timber fibre for paper and board, and biomass or pellet feedstock.

What we handle

Timber only.
That's the whole business.

We don't spread ourselves thin across every waste stream. Timbercycle handles timber — and because that's all we handle, we handle a lot of it. Specialist plant, specialist sorting, and end markets that are already set up to take the output.

  • Timber pallets, all sizes
  • Timber skids and bearers
  • Export crates and dunnage
  • Packing timber and broken stock
  • Offcuts and timber waste
  • Oversize timber for chipping

Treated, painted, laminated and engineered timbers are assessed case by case. We do not accept asbestos or hazardous waste.
