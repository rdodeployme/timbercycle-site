Why here

Pallets arrive. They don't leave.

Short haul is the whole game in timber recovery. Bulky material is expensive to move and cheap to process, so the closer a site is to the yard, the better the numbers work for everyone. Geelong businesses get the benefit of that directly.

That applies to supply as well as collection. Reconditioned pallets, mulch and oversize chip all come out of this site, so local buyers aren't paying to cart material across the state.

Who we collect from in Geelong

If it's timber, it's ours.

  • Manufacturing and food processing
  • Ports, freight and transport operators
  • Builders, renovators and demolition
  • Warehousing and distribution
  • Local councils and transfer stations
  • Businesses buying mulch or pallets

Every collection goes through the same two streams: sound pallets are repaired and returned to the supply chain, and everything past repair is shredded into mulch, fibre and biomass feedstock.

Nothing we collect is sent to landfill as timber.
