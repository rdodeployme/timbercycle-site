Pallet collection in
Truganina & Derrimut.

The western distribution corridor moves an enormous amount of stock on timber. Truganina and Derrimut are full of large-format DCs where pallets accumulate faster than anyone has time to think about them.

Why here

Pallets arrive. They don't leave.

A distribution centre receives palletised stock and ships smaller, mixed orders out. The pallets don't leave with the freight. In a big DC that imbalance builds up quickly, and the usual answer is a bin, a compactor or a corner of the yard nobody wants to look at.

A scheduled program sized to your inbound flow fixes it permanently. Cages or trailers sit where your team already stacks pallets, collections run on a set rhythm, and you get diversion figures out the other end.

Who we collect from in Truganina & Derrimut

If it's timber, it's ours.

  • Large-format distribution centres
  • Retail and e-commerce fulfilment
  • Third-party logistics providers
  • Food and beverage distribution
  • Bulk storage and cross-dock facilities
  • Multi-site operators across the corridor

Every collection goes through the same two streams: sound pallets are repaired and returned to the supply chain, and everything past repair is shredded into mulch, fibre and biomass feedstock.

Nothing we collect is sent to landfill as timber.
