Timbercycle › Your industry › Freight & 3PL

One contractor for every
pallet on every site.

Logistics operators handle other people's freight on other people's pallets, and end up owning the timber nobody wants back. Across several depots, that's a real cost line and a real space problem.

Build a program Book a site assessment
The situation

The pile is predictable. That's the opportunity.

The 3PL pallet problem is a multiplication problem. One depot with a manageable pile becomes six depots with six piles, six bin contracts, and six different arrangements for what happens to the good ones. Nobody has the full picture and nobody is accountable for the number.

One program across every site fixes the accounting as much as the yard. Same equipment, same collection rhythm, one point of contact, and one report that shows what was reused and recycled across the whole network.

Who this is for

Freight & 3PL

  • Freight forwarders and customs brokers
  • Line-haul and transport depots
  • Third-party logistics providers
  • Cross-dock and consolidation facilities
  • Cold chain and bonded storage
  • Operators with sites across Melbourne and regional Victoria
