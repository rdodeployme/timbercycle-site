Pallet collection in
Dandenong.

Dandenong South and the surrounding south-east are manufacturing country. Raw materials and components arrive on pallets, get consumed on site, and leave the timber behind.

Why here

Pallets arrive. They don't leave.

Production sites have a particular pallet problem: the inbound flow is constant but nothing goes back out on timber. Add heavier custom and export pallets, machinery crating and offcuts, and the pile builds faster and heavier than a standard waste service is set up to handle.

We take the whole mix — standard, oversize, custom, crating and broken stock — and sort it at our end. Your team doesn't have to separate anything.

Who we collect from in Dandenong

If it's timber, it's ours.

  • Manufacturing and engineering
  • Automotive and parts distribution
  • Food production and packaging
  • Plastics, steel and building products
  • Machinery importers and crating
  • Industrial and trade wholesalers

Every collection goes through the same two streams: sound pallets are repaired and returned to the supply chain, and everything past repair is shredded into mulch, fibre and biomass feedstock.

Nothing we collect is sent to landfill as timber.
