Questions

Straight answers.

The things people actually ask before they hand over a pallet stream. If yours isn't here, call us.

What does it cost?

It depends on your pallets, and we'd rather say that than quote a number we can't hold. Volume, condition mix, location and how full each collection runs all move the price. Some sites cost us very little to service and some cost a lot. Send us your volumes and we'll model it properly.

What pallet sizes do you take?

All of them. Australian standard, Euro, export and custom-built, plus timber skids, bearers, export crates and dunnage. Oversize and odd-shaped timber is fine — it goes to the shredder rather than the repair bench.

Do you take broken pallets?

Yes. Broken pallets are the point of Stream 02. They get shredded into mulch, fibre and feedstock rather than buried. You don't need to separate good from broken — that's our job, not yours.

Do we have to sort them first?

No. Your team stacks pallets where they already stack them. We collect the lot and grade at our end.

Do you take treated or painted timber?

We assess it case by case. Treated, painted, laminated and engineered timbers need careful handling and the pathways aren't as clean, so we'll tell you honestly what we can and can't do with a given load.

Do you take asbestos or hazardous waste?

No. We do not accept asbestos or hazardous waste under any circumstances.

What actually happens to the pallets?

Sound ones are repaired and go back into the supply chain. Everything else is shredded and becomes recycled mulch, oversize chip, timber fibre for paper and board, or biomass and pellet feedstock.

Can we get reporting for our sustainability numbers?

Yes. We record volumes collected, reused and diverted from landfill per site and per period, so the figures in your reporting have something behind them.

How quickly can you start?

It depends on your volume and where you are. A site assessment is the first step — call 13 44 55 and we'll tell you honestly what the lead time looks like.

Where do you collect from?

Victoria. Our yard is in North Geelong and we run across Melbourne metro and regional Victoria. Other states are planned but not yet servicing, and we won't claim otherwise.

Do you sell pallets as well as collect them?

Yes. Reconditioned pallets are graded and sold back into the market, along with mulch, fibre and feedstock. If you buy pallets regularly it's worth asking us first.

Still got a question?

Call us. A two-minute conversation usually beats a form.
