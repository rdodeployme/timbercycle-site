Melbourne runs on palletised freight. Almost none of it goes back where it came from. We collect timber pallets right across the metro area and give every one of them somewhere to go.

Why here

Pallets arrive. They don't leave.

Melbourne's warehousing, freight and manufacturing sit in bands right around the city — the western corridor, the south-east, the north. Pallets arrive at all of them with inbound freight and then stop moving. Most sites end up paying for general waste bins to shift timber that is worth something to somebody.

If you run more than one Melbourne site, this works better still: the same program, the same schedule and one point of contact across all of them, instead of a different waste arrangement at every address.

Who we collect from in Melbourne

If it's timber, it's ours.

  • Warehouses and distribution centres
  • Freight forwarders and 3PL operators
  • Manufacturing and production sites
  • Retail and wholesale distribution
  • Construction and demolition projects
  • Transfer stations and recycling centres

Every collection goes through the same two streams: sound pallets are repaired and returned to the supply chain, and everything past repair is shredded into mulch, fibre and biomass feedstock.

Nothing we collect is sent to landfill as timber.
