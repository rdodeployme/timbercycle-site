Stream 01

Pallet recovery
and reuse.

Most pallets that get thrown out are still structurally sound. We collect them, grade them, repair what's worth repairing, and put them back into service.

The principle

Reuse beats recycling. Every time.

Shredding a sound pallet destroys the work that went into building it. Repairing it keeps that value in the system, costs the next user less than buying new, and takes a bin out of your yard either way.

So reuse is the first question we ask of every pallet that comes through. Recycling is what happens when the answer is no.

The grade

Every pallet gets one of three answers.

Grading is the whole business in miniature. It's done at our yard, by people who look at pallets all day, and it decides which stream a pallet goes down.

A

Sound

Structurally intact, safe to load. Inspected, cleaned and back out the door as a reconditioned pallet.

Stream 01 · straight back to work
B

Repairable

A broken board, a split bearer, loose fixings. Repaired at the bench, re-graded, and back into service.

Stream 01 · via the repair bench
C

Beyond repair

Not worth the bench time, or not safe to load again. Shredded into mulch, fibre and feedstock. Never landfill.

Stream 02 · shred
How it works

From your yard, back to work.

  1. Collect

    Scheduled pickups or on-call collection, with cages, trailers or bins on site depending on how fast your pallets accumulate. No sorting required at your end.

  2. Sort and grade

    Every pallet is assessed for structural integrity and safety, then graded. Sound pallets go to reuse, borderline ones to repair, the rest to Stream 02.

  3. Repair and rebuild

    Broken boards and bearers replaced, loose fixings made good, rebuilt from recovered timber where it makes sense. Back to a pallet you'd be comfortable loading.

  4. Back into supply chains

    Graded, reconditioned pallets go back out to businesses that would rather buy recovered than new — usually at a lower cost, and without waiting on new stock.

  5. Nothing wasted

    Anything beyond safe repair moves straight into Stream 02 for shredding. It never becomes a landfill problem, and it never becomes yours.
