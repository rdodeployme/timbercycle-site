The problem

Pallets are the waste stream nobody owns.

They arrive with freight, they don't leave with it, and they end up stacked against a fence until someone books a bin. Then good pallets and broken pallets go to landfill together, and the site pays for the privilege.

It's a solvable problem. It just needs one operator who wants all of them — the good ones and the wrecked ones — and has somewhere for both to go.

How a program runs

Set it up once. It runs itself.

How it runs

  1. Site assessment

    We look at your volumes, pallet types, condition mix and where they accumulate, then size the program to match. No guessing from a phone call.

  2. Equipment on site

    Cages, trailers or bins positioned where your team already stacks pallets, so nobody has to change how they work.

  3. Scheduled collections

    A set run that keeps up with your inbound flow, with on-call pickups when a big intake lands. Your yard stops filling up.

  4. Grading and processing

    Everything is graded back at our site. Sound pallets are repaired and resold; the rest are shredded into mulch, fibre and feedstock.

  5. Reporting back

    Volumes collected, reused and diverted from landfill, recorded per site — so your sustainability reporting has real numbers behind it.

Commercials

Priced against your actual pallets.

What a program costs — or returns — depends on how many pallets you generate, what condition they're in, and how much of the mix is genuinely reusable. A site producing sound, standard pallets is a very different proposition to one producing broken oversize timber, and we price it that way.

Give us the volumes and we'll model it against your site before anyone signs anything.

One contractor

Every pallet on site handled by one operator, not split across a waste contractor and whoever will take the good ones.

No sorting

Your team stacks pallets. That's the whole job. Grading happens at our end, not yours.

Multi-site

Run the same program across several locations with consistent scheduling and one point of contact.

Diversion data

Reporting that shows what was reused and what was recycled, per site and per period.
