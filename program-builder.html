Proposed operation

  • Collection: Weekly collection, adjusted after the first month against actual volumes.
  • Equipment: Pallet cages or a trailer per site.
  • Your side: stack pallets where you already stack them. No sorting, no separating good from broken.
  • Our side: collect, grade at our North Geelong yard, repair sound pallets for return to service, shred the rest into mulch, fibre and feedstock.
  • Reporting: pallets and tonnes collected, reused and recycled, per site, per period.

Commercial structure

  • Modelled against these volumes and your actual pallet mix following a site assessment.
  • A trial period with a sample-load grade, so the reusable share in the agreement is the real one.

Next steps

  • Site assessment booked at your convenience.
  • Equipment positioned and first collection scheduled.
  • First-month review of volumes and reusable share.

Figures are estimates from the volumes entered, assuming an average pallet weight of 25 kg. Not accepted: asbestos and hazardous waste.
