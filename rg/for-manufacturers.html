Timbercycle › Your industry › Manufacturers

Raw materials arrive on timber.

Production sites consume what arrives and keep the timber it came on. Add machinery crating, heavy custom pallets and offcuts from the floor, and the pile builds faster and heavier than a standard waste service is set up for.

Build a program Book a site assessment
The situation

The pile is predictable. That's the opportunity.

Manufacturing timber is the mixed case: standard pallets alongside oversize custom ones, export-grade hardwood alongside broken softwood, crating, bearers and offcuts. General waste contractors price that by the bin and send it all to the same place.

We take the whole mix and sort it at our end. Sound pallets are repaired and returned to service, heavy custom timber is assessed for reuse, and everything else is shredded into mulch, fibre and feedstock. Nothing you send us goes to landfill as timber.

Who this is for

Manufacturers

  • Food and beverage production
  • Engineering and metal fabrication
  • Plastics, packaging and chemicals
  • Building products and joinery
  • Machinery importers and assemblers
  • Automotive and component manufacturing
Why us for this

Timber only. All of it.

  • Mixed loads are normal. Custom, oversize and crating are all fine.
  • Treated and painted timber assessed case by case rather than refused outright.
  • On-site processing for sites producing serious offcut volume.
  • One collection for all of it instead of separate arrangements per material.
