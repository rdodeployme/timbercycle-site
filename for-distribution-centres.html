The situation

The pile is predictable. That's the opportunity.

Distribution centres have the cleanest version of the pallet problem: a constant, predictable inbound flow and almost nothing going back out on timber. That predictability is exactly what makes a scheduled program work — we can size the equipment and the collection rhythm to your receiving volume and hold it there.

It also means DC pallets tend to be in good condition. A high share of them can be repaired and returned to service rather than shredded, which changes the economics of the whole program in your favour.
